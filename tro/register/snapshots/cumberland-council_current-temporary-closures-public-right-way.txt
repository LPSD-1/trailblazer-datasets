Current temporary closures of a public right of way
A list of current orders in place for the closure of public right of ways in the area.
Temporary Traffic Regulation Orders (TTRO) can be made for footpaths, bridleways and byways for up to 6 months initially (18 months for all other highways).
If you are carrying out works on or affecting a public right of way you need to apply for a Temporary Closure Order.
